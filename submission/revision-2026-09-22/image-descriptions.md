# Image descriptions

## Figure 1

Scatterplot of mean Jaccard distance to personal-pronoun anchors on the horizontal axis and compound-determinative anchors on the vertical axis. Blue circles show personal-pronoun controls, red squares show compound controls, and grey triangles show the reciprocals. Each control is excluded from its own pool. A diagonal dashed line marks equal distances. All pronoun controls are above it and all compound controls below it. The reciprocals lie below the line, between the two control groups: *each other* at about 0.71 and 0.67, and *one another* at about 0.72 and 0.68. Each is nearer each group than the other group's controls are, but farther from each group than its own controls, except dummy *it*.

## Figure 2

Six histograms show fixed-anchor Jaccard reference distributions: *each other* in the top row and *one another* in the bottom row. Columns show distance to personal pronouns, distance to compound determinatives, and their difference, Δ. Each row uses 20,000 randomized profiles preserving that target's total number of positive features. Blue vertical lines mark observed values. In both distance columns the observed line is left of every simulated value. In the contrast column the observed values lie near the 90th and 93rd percentiles. Low distance tails under this benchmark also occur commonly among the controls.

## Figure 3

Two panels show the reference percentile of Δ for *each other* and *one another*. The horizontal axis lists all features, no morphology, no syntax, no semantics, and no phonology. Blue circles denote Jaccard, red squares Dice, green triangles Hamming, and purple diamonds IDF-weighted Jaccard. The vertical scale runs from 0 to 1; a dashed line marks the median reference contrast at 0.5. All-feature percentiles range from about 0.75 to 0.96. Removing morphology puts every measure near the lower end; removing semantics puts them near the upper end. The panels use full comparator pools, item holdout, and fixed-total placement.
