# JQL Image Descriptions

These optional descriptions can be used if the Taylor & Francis submission portal invites author-supplied image descriptions.

## Figure 1

Multiple Correspondence Analysis plot of the 155-property grammatical feature matrix. Pronouns and determinatives form partially separated regions in the two-dimensional projection. Compound determinatives sit near the region where the two categories approach each other. The two reciprocals, *each other* and *one another*, are plotted as triangles in that interface region rather than deep inside either anchor category.

## Figure 2

Histogram of 5,000 quasiswap reference values for the distance contrast Delta under a row- and column-preserving reference model. Most reference values cluster near zero. The observed value is marked by a dashed vertical line far into the positive tail, showing that the observed determinative-ward contrast is unusual under this constrained benchmark.

## Figure 3

Specification curve for Delta across combinations of distance metric, feature-block exclusion, and regularization method. Most specifications remain on the positive side of zero, meaning the reciprocals are closer to compound determinatives than to pronouns. The morphology-exclusion panel is the main exception, where the contrast turns pronoun-ward.

## Figure 4

Histogram of Delta values across 100 rotations of six pronoun and six compound-determinative comparison items. The prespecified comparison set is marked by a dashed red line near the high end of the rotation distribution. The rotation median is lower, and the interquartile range spans smaller positive contrasts, showing that the magnitude of Delta is sensitive to comparator choice even though most rotations remain determinative-ward.

## Figure 5

Predictive blending curves for *each other* and *one another*. The x-axis is the pronoun-profile mixture weight, and the y-axis is predictive loss. Each curve reaches its minimum near 0.5, with best-fitting weights of 0.504 for *each other* and 0.451 for *one another*, indicating near-parity between pronoun-like and compound-determinative-like profiles.

## Figure 6

Two posterior predictive check histograms for the measurement workflow. The first shows Procrustes statistics for replicated datasets with the observed statistic marked by a vertical line, indicating that the observed global geometry is plausible under replication. The second shows nearest-neighbour overlap around anchors, again with the observed value marked, indicating that replicated datasets preserve substantial local neighbourhood structure.

## Figure 7

Reliability diagram for cross-validated pronoun-label probabilities. Predicted probabilities are plotted against observed frequencies, and the curve tracks the diagonal reasonably closely. The pattern indicates approximate calibration: when the model assigns a given probability to the pronoun label, the observed frequency of pronouns among held-out items is similar.
